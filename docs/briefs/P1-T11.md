## What main changed first, and why

The bus could not carry a transcript honestly before this task:

- **No tokens.** `Reply` streamed the model but only emitted sentences and audio, so nothing
  could stream text or time a first token. It now emits `token`, and `VoiceSession` puts
  `assistant.token` on the bus.
- **Words that could be taken back.** A model-ended turn is provisional until `confirmedAt`
  (ADR-25), but `user.transcript` went on the bus before that — a panel would have shown a
  line and then had to remove it. `VoiceSession` now **holds the transcript, tokens and
  sentences until the turn is confirmed** and publishes them stamped with when they
  happened. A retracted turn publishes none of them.
- **No way to type.** `user.message` existed in the protocol and the machine, but nothing
  answered it. `ChatSession` (`packages/core/src/chat`) does: text in, tokens out, stop,
  history.

## What to build

### 1. The reducer — `packages/core/src/transcript/`

`transcript.ts` with `TranscriptLine` (the shape in the note), `TranscriptState`
(`{ lines, …whatever bookkeeping you need }`), `emptyTranscript()`,
`reduceTranscript(state, event): TranscriptState` (pure, returns a new state, never mutates)
and `transcriptToText(lines, characterName): string` for Copy. Export from
`packages/core/src/index.ts`. **Every row of the note's event table is a test**, plus: the
full voice barge-in sequence (`assistant.interrupted` → `assistant.message` with a prefix →
`state.changed` to `listening`) keeps one interrupted line; an abandoned voice reply (tokens,
then `state.changed` to `listening`, no message) leaves no line; a stopped chat reply with
empty text leaves no line; latency from `user.turn.ended` and from `user.message`, and null
after `user.turn.resumed`; a `nothing was recognised` error is not a line; a backchannel is
not a line.

Drive at least one test with the real `ChatSession` over a scripted LLM, feeding the
machine's events through the reducer, so the two cannot drift apart.

### 2. The panel — `apps/web/src/transcript/TranscriptPanel.tsx`

Props: `lines: readonly TranscriptLine[]`, `characterName: string`, `onClear: () => void`, and
whatever the copy status needs. Rendering, badges, interrupted styling, notices, scrolling,
the `log` region, Copy and Clear exactly as the note says. Keep the reducer out of the
component: a small hook `useTranscript(subscribe)` that takes a `(listener) => unsubscribe`
function and returns `{ lines, clear }` lets both pages share it.

### 3. `/chat` — `apps/web/src/chat/ChatPage.tsx`

A production route in `App.tsx` (`CHAT_PATH = '/chat'`, `lazy()` like `/settings`), linked from
the boot screen next to Settings. On load: `loadSettings()`; without an endpoint and a model,
show a short explanation and a link to `/settings`, nothing else. Otherwise one
`ConversationMachine` + `ChatSession` per page, the panel, and the text box from the note
(Enter/Shift+Enter, Stop while `busy`, focus kept). The key is loaded from the vault and the
transport resolved **once per send** rather than at page load, so saving a key in another tab
works. A transport failure (companion not running) becomes a notice line: dispatch an `error`
event with `scope: 'llm'` and the `resolveTransport` result's text on the machine. Character
name: `'Alice'` (ADR-18) until personas exist.

### 4. The harness — `/dev/voice`

Give `VoicePipeline` (`apps/web/src/dev/voice-pipeline.ts`) an
`onConversation(listener: (event: ConversationEvent) => void): () => void` that forwards the
machine's events (the machine is created in `wire()`; subscribe listeners there, and keep
them across a restart). Mount the panel in `VoiceHarness.tsx` beside the existing tables.
Change nothing else in the harness.

### 5. Styling

`theme.css`, tokens only, `transcript-*` and `chat-*` classes; `theme.test.ts` must pass. Works
at ~400 px.
