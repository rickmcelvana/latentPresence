## Decisions already taken, so you do not have to

**1. Two new modules, split the way the package already splits.**
- `packages/avatar/src/stage/camera.ts` — **pure and three-free**: presets and the rig.
  Exported from the package root like `gaze.ts`.
- `packages/avatar/src/vrm/stage.ts` — **three.js**: `buildStage(options)` returns a
  `THREE.Group` with the room and the lights. Exported from `@latentpresence/avatar/vrm`.

**2. Four presets: `face`, `bust`, `medium`, `full`.** The plan names three; `face` is the
close-up R-11 used to judge lip sync, and it replaces `setCameraDistance`, which you
**remove** (its doc says it is a stand-in for this task). Each preset frames a vertical span
of the body, taken from the loaded model's bones rather than hard-coded heights:

| preset | top of frame | bottom of frame |
|---|---|---|
| `face` | a little above the head bone | a little below the chin (head − ~0.12 m) |
| `bust` | above the head | the chest (`upperChest`, else `chest`) |
| `medium` | above the head | the hips |
| `full` | above the head | the feet (`leftFoot`/`rightFoot`, else y = 0) |

Pure function, something like `framing(preset, body, fovDegrees) → { position: Vec3; target:
Vec3 }` where `body` is the bone heights plus the character's x/z. Fixed vertical FOV (30°,
as now); distance = half the span / tan(fov/2), times a margin (~1.1). The camera stays at
the eyes' height or a touch below for `face`/`bust` (a call is roughly eye level) and can
drop towards the span's centre for `full`. The character stands at the origin facing **+Z**
(VRM 1.0), so the camera sits on +Z looking back.

**3. Transitions are eased and interruptible.** `CameraRig` (pure): `setGoal(framing,
transitionMs)` and `update(deltaMs) → { position, target }`. It eases position and target
from **wherever it currently is** — a new goal mid-transition starts from the current pose,
never from the previous preset, so there is no jump. Smoothstep over `transitionMs` is fine.
`transitionMs = 0` snaps. Default 700 ms.

**4. The renderer owns a stage and a rig.**
- Constructor option `stage?: StageOptions | false` (default: a stage). `false` gives the
  bare scene — keep a little ambient light so a model is still visible.
- The three lights currently built in the renderer's constructor **move into the stage**
  (the stage is the lighting now).
- New methods: `setCameraPreset(preset, transitionMs?)`, `setShadows(on: boolean)`,
  `setPixelRatio(ratio: number)`. Remove `setCameraDistance`.
- `frameHead()` becomes "compute the body from the bones, give the rig its goal"; `update`
  steps the rig and applies it to the camera before rendering. The gaze placement already
  reads the camera position — keep it reading the camera's *current* position.
- On `loadCharacter`, set `castShadow` on the character's meshes.
- The default surface enables `shadowMap` (PCF soft). Extend `DrawingSurface` minimally if
  you need to (e.g. an optional `shadowMap` field); tests pass a fake surface.

**5. The room is built from primitives — no downloaded assets, no textures, nothing
fetched.** We ship nothing we cannot redistribute, and a stage that downloads is a stage
that needs a consent screen. `MeshStandardMaterial` in muted colours, all of them in one
exported `STAGE_PALETTE` object in `stage.ts` (the no-colour-literals rule is about CSS,
but keep scene colours in one place in the same spirit). Contents:
- a floor; a back wall ~2–2.5 m behind her; optionally one side wall;
- a **window** in the back wall — a frame plus a bright (emissive) pane reading as
  daylight; it does not need to be a real hole;
- a **desk** — top and legs — placed to her side and behind her, so that **it never sits
  between the camera and her body in any preset**. A test proves this (below).
Name every part (`floor`, `back-wall`, `window`, `desk`, …) so tests and debugging can find it.

**6. Three-point lighting**, named `key`, `fill`, `rim`:
- `key`: `DirectionalLight`, warm, front-left and high; **the only shadow caster**, shadow
  map 1024², a shadow camera fitted tightly around her (not the default huge frustum).
- `fill`: weaker, cooler, front-right; no shadows.
- `rim`: behind and above, to separate her from the wall; no shadows.
- plus a low `HemisphereLight` so nothing goes black (MToon needs some ambient).
`StageOptions` has at least `shadows: boolean` (default `true`). Floor and walls receive
shadows.

**7. No post-processing.** No `EffectComposer`, no bloom, nothing added to the render call.
Do not change tone mapping or output colour space.

**8. The debug page gets the controls and the instrument** (in the existing Lip sync panel's
**Camera** select, or a new **Stage** panel — your call, but one place):
- camera preset select: face / bust / medium / full (replaces bust / close-up);
- shadows on/off;
- render scale: `native` or `1080p` — for 1080p, `setPixelRatio(1920 / canvas.clientWidth)`,
  so the drawing buffer is 1920 px wide whatever the column is;
- a frame-rate readout from `FrameRecorder` (`apps/web/src/spikes/frame-rate.ts`): mark every
  rendered frame; a **Take reading** button shows median fps, 5th-percentile fps and the
  worst frame, plus the canvas's real backing size (`canvas.width × canvas.height`) — read,
  not derived; and a **Reset** that starts a new window. Changing preset, shadows or scale
  resets it, because a reading is only meaningful for one configuration.
Every new className gets a rule in `apps/web/src/theme.css`, using the existing tokens.

## Tests you write

- `stage/camera.test.ts`: every preset's span fits the vertical FOV (top and bottom of the
  span inside the view from the computed position); distances increase face < bust < medium
  < full; target is centred on the span; the camera is on +Z of the character.
- Rig tests: reaches the goal at `transitionMs`; the first frame after `setGoal` moves only a
  little (eased, not a jump); a new goal mid-transition continues from the current pose (no
  frame-to-frame jump larger than a normal step); `0` snaps.
- `vrm/stage.test.ts` (node — three's scene graph needs no WebGL): the named parts and
  lights exist; `key.castShadow === shadows`; `fill`/`rim` never cast; **the desk's bounding
  box does not intersect a standing-person box** (x ∈ [−0.35, 0.35], z ∈ [−0.3, 0.3],
  y ∈ [0, 1.8]); **and for every preset, segments from the preset's camera position to the
  head, hips and feet of a standard body do not pass through the desk's box**
  (`THREE.Ray.intersectsBox`, or segment-vs-box).
- Update `renderer`-level tests if any break; `FakeAvatarRenderer` is unaffected (camera
  presets are not on the `AvatarRenderer` interface).
