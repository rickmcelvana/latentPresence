# P4-T07 — Planning studio: the Plans panel (brief)

**Owner:** sub for the panel (a subagent the architect runs in-session); main for the rest,
already in the tree: `packages/core/src/plan` (`Plans`, `planTools`, `planToMarkdown`,
`renderPlans`), the prompt section, `PromptContext.plans`, the kernel's `recordFact`/`closeFact`,
and the wiring in `ChatPage.tsx` (search "P4-T07"). ADR-41 (proposed) says why.
**Done when:** "let's plan a vegetable garden" ends with a saved plan visible in the panel and
in MariaDB. The architect proves the MariaDB half live; this brief is the panel half.

You are building one React panel and wiring it into `/chat`'s drawer, over an API that already
exists. Read this brief, then the files it names; do not redesign what they decide. **Never
edit `packages/protocol`, `packages/core` or `companion/`, never commit.** If the API looks
wrong or missing for what the brief asks, stop and say so in your report rather than working
around it.

## What to build

### 1. Wiring in `ChatPage.tsx`

- `drawerView` gains `'plans'`; a **Plans** button beside Memory, same toggle rule and
  `aria-pressed` rule. The drawer's default is unchanged (transcript).
- Render `<PlansPanel plans={plans} characterName={CHAT_CHARACTER_NAME} />` for that view.

### 2. `apps/web/src/plan/PlansPanel.tsx`

Props: `plans: Plans | null`, `characterName: string`. Read the snapshot with
`useSyncExternalStore(plans.subscribe, plans.snapshot)` (bind or wrap — they are methods).

- **Off:** with `plans` null, one sentence that plans are kept with memory, and Settings →
  Memory turns it on (link `/settings`), like the memory panel's.
- **List view** (the default): header "Plans". Each unarchived plan as a row button: title,
  status pill, "3 of 9 done" (dropped tasks not counted), updated date; the plan in focus
  marked ("working on it now" or similar). Archived plans behind a "Show archived" toggle.
  Follow-ups (`snapshot.followUps`) under the plan they name, as "Follow up on 15 Oct: …"
  (match by `planSubject(plan.title) === followUp.subject`; `planSubject` is exported). Empty
  state: "No plans yet. Say "let's plan…" and she will start one."
- **Opening a plan** shows the editor and calls `plans.setFocus(plan.id)` — the person opening
  it is how they point her at it (ADR-41). **Back** returns to the list (focus stays).
- **Editor:** a local draft copied from the plan, and the version it was opened at.
  - Title (input), goal (textarea), status (select).
  - Each phase: title input, remove; its tasks: status select, title input, remove, and notes
    (a small textarea, collapsed behind a "Notes" toggle when empty). **Add task** per phase,
    **Add phase** at the end. New phases and tasks get ids from `crypto.randomUUID()`.
  - **Save** (disabled until the draft differs) → `plans.save(draft, openedVersion)`. On
    success the draft and opened version follow the saved plan. On refusal with `current`,
    show `reason` and two buttons: **Keep mine** (save the draft again against
    `current.version`) and **Load hers** (discard the draft, open `current`).
  - **Revert** (discard the draft).
  - **While not editing**, a change from her (the snapshot's plan with this id has a newer
    version) replaces the draft silently; **while editing**, show a one-line notice that she
    changed it, with **Load hers** — never overwrite a draft the person is typing into.
  - **Export:** **Download .md** (a `Blob` + an `<a download={planFileName(plan)}>` click;
    exports the saved plan, not the draft) and **Copy Markdown** (`navigator.clipboard.writeText`;
    say "Copied" or the failure, like the transcript's copy).
- Busy state on Save; a failure's message in a line under the editor, the rest intact.

### 3. Tests — `apps/web/src/plan/PlansPanel.test.tsx`

With a real `Plans` over `FakeMemoryStore` (and a `MemoryKernel` for follow-ups), no mocks of
the API: off state; list shows plans with progress and the focus mark, archived hidden until
asked; opening a plan sets the focus; edit → Save writes version + 1 to the store; her
`plans.revise` while the draft is clean updates the editor, while dirty shows the notice and
leaves the draft; a conflicting Save offers Keep mine (saves over) and Load hers (discards);
add/remove phase and task; follow-up shown under its plan; Download builds the Markdown of the
saved plan (stub `URL.createObjectURL`); Copy writes it. In `ChatPage.test.tsx`: the Plans
button switches the drawer's view with `aria-pressed`, and **a scripted model that calls
`plan_create` during a typed message leaves the plan in the store and in the Plans panel** —
the done-when through the page.
