# P9-T01 Accessibility and reduced motion — brief

PLAN.md's Phase 9 names three things: **captions, keyboard control, reduced motion**. This task
does those three and a sweep that makes the rest checkable by machine. ADR-67 (proposed)
records what reduced motion and captions mean for a character on a video call.

## Split

| Part | Owner | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Captions | main | `packages/core/src/captions/`, `apps/web/src/call/Captions.tsx`, `ChatPage.tsx`, `theme.css` |
| B. Reduced motion | main | `theme.css` |
| C. Call shortcuts | main | `ChatPage.tsx` |
| D. Automated checks and the fixes they find | sub | component files and their tests, **not** `ChatPage.tsx` or `theme.css` |

## A. Captions (main)

What she **says aloud**, on the stage, as the words are heard — for a person who cannot hear
her or the room is loud. A pure reducer in core (`reduceCaptions`), like `reduceTranscript`:

- A sentence's text is kept by index on `assistant.sentence`; it becomes the caption on that
  index's `assistant.audio.started`, so the caption changes with the voice, not ahead of it.
- With no voice out (typed, Speak replies off) there is no caption: nothing is heard, and the
  transcript has the words. A settled answer (`assistant.message`) drops unplayed sentences.
- Barge-in (`assistant.interrupted`) clears hers at once: the rest was never said.
- Their own final `user.transcript` shows as theirs ("You"), so they can see what was heard.
  Typed messages are not captioned (they can see what they typed). Backchannels are not (ADR-28).
- A caption stays `CAPTION_HOLD_MS` after its audio ends, then goes.
- A **Captions** toggle in the call bar, remembered on this device; off by default. The caption
  is not a live region: the transcript's `log` is the screen reader's channel, and announcing
  both would read each sentence twice, over her voice.

## B. Reduced motion (main)

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` turns off every CSS animation and transition in one rule, in
place of the three blocks that each covered one class. Her own movement stays: she is the
content, as a person on a video call is; the call's camera does not move (one preset, set once
with no transition — `CallStage`'s mount). Recorded in ADR-67.

## C. Call shortcuts (main)

On `/chat`, with focus outside a text field and no modifier held: **M** mute, **C** captions,
**T** the text box, **Escape** closes the drawer. Each button names its key (`aria-keyshortcuts`
and its `title`).

## D. Automated checks (sub)

1. **axe-core in the unit tests.** Add `axe-core` (MPL-2.0) as a dev dependency of `apps/web`
   (check `pnpm-workspace.yaml`'s diff after the add — pnpm writes release-age exclusions; pin a
   version at least a day old). A helper `expectAccessible(container)` in
   `apps/web/src/test/axe.ts` runs `axe.run` on a rendered container with `color-contrast`
   disabled (jsdom has no layout or canvas; contrast is checked by step 2) and fails listing
   each violation's id, help and target. Call it from the existing test file of every
   component under `apps/web/src` except `dev/`, `spikes/` and `ChatPage`, in one test per
   file that renders its main state. Fix what it finds in the component.
2. **Contrast of the tokens.** `apps/web/src/contrast.test.ts` reads `:root` in `theme.css`,
   and for each text/background pair the stylesheet actually uses (list them in the test, with
   where each is used) asserts the WCAG 2.2 ratio: 4.5 for body text, 3 for large text and for
   focus rings and control borders against their background. Do **not** change a token to make
   it pass: report the failing pairs and their ratios.
3. Record axe-core's version, licence and the jsdom limitation in `docs/SURFACE.md` with the date.

Never commit, never touch `packages/protocol`, `ChatPage.tsx` or `theme.css`. Report: files
changed, each violation found and how it was fixed, contrast failures, and `pnpm gate`'s result.

## Done when

- Captions follow her voice on the stage, sentence by sentence, and stop on barge-in; tests in
  core for every rule above and a render test of the toggle.
- With reduced motion set, no CSS animation or transition runs (a theme test checks the rule).
- M, C, T and Escape work in the call and are named on their buttons; tested.
- Every component's test runs axe with no violations; the contrast test passes or its failures
  are an ADR note with Rick's call.
- `pnpm gate` green.
