**latentPresence 1.0 is out.**

It's a companion you talk to face to face. A full-body character joins you in what feels like a
video call: you speak or type, she listens, reacts and answers out loud, with expressions,
gestures and lip sync. She remembers you between visits, plans things with you, reminds you when
you ask, looks things up in your own notes and documents, and can run your smart home through
Home Assistant.

You choose the AI model that does the thinking: Ollama or LM Studio on your own computer, or a
cloud service you already have a key for. latentPresence ships no models, sends nothing to us,
and has no telemetry. Every model it can download is listed with its licence, and nothing
downloads until you agree.

It's open source (Apache-2.0) and free.

- Windows: https://github.com/rickmcelvana/latentPresence/releases/latest
- In Chrome or Edge, on any system: https://app.latentpresence.com
- The guide: https://latentpresence.com/docs/

It's young, so tell us what breaks: https://latentpresence.com/feedback/
