latentPresence 1.1: plugins, and characters you make. Check for updates brings you here from 1.0, and your conversations, memory and settings stay.

New since 1.0:
- Plugins, in Settings → Plugins. Browse the list, or install one from a folder, a .zip or an address, then turn it on for a character. Before anything is installed you see who made it, every host it may contact, what it may download and the tools it adds. Plugins run no code in the app.
- Three plugins in the list. Tarot draws from a full 78-card deck and lays the spread on the wall behind her. Office makes and edits Word documents and spreadsheets in folders you open for writing, with the companion in this app. Wheel of the Year, written by a tester, knows the eight seasonal festivals and which one is coming up.
- Make your own character in Settings → Character: who they are, how they talk, what they will not do, their voice and body. Duplicate Alice or Theo to start from them. Each character remembers you separately, and renaming one keeps it all.
- Export saves a character as a file to share or keep, and Import… brings one in. The file holds the character only: no memories, body, plugins or settings.
- Deleting a character asks whether to keep what they remember, so importing them again brings it back, or to forget it for good.
- Whatever a character says, she tells anyone who sincerely asks that she is an AI. A line telling her to claim to be human gets a warning.
- A long list of bodies no longer pushes the call up over the page.
- Memory in MariaDB: deleting a remembered turn, fact or document could stop her finding other memories by meaning. That is fixed, and on its first start this version rebuilds the search index once, which can take a moment on a large memory.

Known:
- The installer is not code-signed yet: when Windows SmartScreen asks, choose More info, then Run anyway.
- Talking out loud needs a GPU with WebGPU, and the first call downloads about 480 MB of voice models.
- A character you make lives in this app (or this browser) until you export it.
- macOS and Linux have no app yet: use https://app.latentpresence.com in Chrome, and the companion from source for its extras.
